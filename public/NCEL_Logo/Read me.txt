The NCEL logo is available in high resolution in four formats in this zip file.

NCEL Logo.png is the one that is most likely going to be useful as that can be loaded and incorporated into websites and programmes the easiest.

The other three - NCEL Logo.eps, NCEL Logo.pdf and NCEL Logo.svg - are for specific pieces of software such as Illustrator, Photoshop, Adobe Acrobat Reader etc, to help with printing of any size.

Any questions, then please email webmaster@ncel.org.uk.
